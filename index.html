Privacy

Privacy Policy

Caya Scoops N Smile uses customer information to operate ordering, rewards, support, security, and business reporting.

Information Collected

Name, email, phone number, date of birth, address, favorite flavors, loyalty ID, points, rewards, order history, support messages, and payment/order status may be stored.

How It Is Used

Information is used for account setup, checkout, receipts, order updates, loyalty rewards, birthday treats, referral rewards, gift cards, customer support, fraud prevention, and business reports.

Service Providers

The app uses Firebase for authentication/database services, EmailJS for email messages, Netlify for hosting, WhatsApp links for support/order communication, and WiPay or approved payment links for payment handling.

Security

Admin and cashier access is limited to business use. Activity logs, auto logout, role controls, and security rules help protect customer and business records.

Retention & Deletion

Records may be kept for order history, rewards, taxes, customer service, fraud prevention, and business accounting. Customers may request account or data review by contacting the business.

Refund Policy

Refunds & Order Issues

Refunds, voids, replacements, and order corrections are reviewed by management. Keep your receipt or order reference for faster help.

Order Issues

Contact us as soon as possible if an item is incorrect, missing, damaged, or not prepared as expected.

Fresh Food

Because items are fresh and perishable, refunds are not automatic after pickup. Management may offer replacement, store credit, reward adjustment, or refund depending on the issue.

Payment Review

Refunds are reviewed based on order status, payment confirmation, pickup status, customer communication, and supporting details.

Gift Cards

Gift cards are activated only after payment confirmation. Used balances cannot be refunded except where required by law or approved by management.

Promos & Rewards

Points, birthday rewards, referral rewards, and promo codes may be adjusted if an order is refunded, voided, cancelled, or corrected.
